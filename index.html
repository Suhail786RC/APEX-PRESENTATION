08 / One process · Two IT solutions

Two routes to a controlled workflow.

Both options must support the supplier certification lifecycle and its priority requirements.

A

Central workflow platform

Standard records, reviews and renewal rules.

Trade-off: product fit and platform limits require verification.

Preferred direction
B

Low-code case management

Tailored case forms, stages and work queues.

Trade-off: more configuration and ongoing ownership.

Comparison of two solution approaches across process fit, control, feasibility, maintainability and cost.
Decision factorWeightA · Central workflowB · Low-code cases
Process fit30%Standard workflow must fit the lifecycleTailor forms and case stages
Control & traceability25%Verify built-in versions, roles and audit trailDesign and test configured controls
Delivery feasibility20%Less design effort if standard features fitMore configuration and governance
Maintainability15%Assess vendor support and product limitsAssign application and rule ownership
Total cost10%Licence and configuration quote neededLicence, configuration and support quote needed

RecommendationA central workflow platform, subject to verified fit, controls and total cost.

09 / Conclusion & recommendation

In conclusion.

We recommend a central workflow platform to connect supplier evidence, human approval and renewal monitoring.

01

Centralise supplier evidence

Keep a unique supplier identity, linked audit and lab evidence, and earlier document versions.

02

Keep decisions accountable

Assign review ownership, retain human approval and record the decision history.

03

Make renewal risks actionable

Connect configured reminders to a dashboard that shows outstanding evidence and expiry exceptions.

The key takeaway

One record.
Clear decisions.
Visible renewal risks.

Centralise routine work so compliance officers can focus on evidence and approval.
